About

Made for people who know ball.

NBA Minigames is a free collection of daily puzzles and quick games built on real NBA stats. No account, no ads, no download. Your streaks and scores stay on your own device.

Who made it

Built by mdivs2121, a basketball fan who wanted games that test what you actually know about the league. Ideas and bug reports are always welcome.

The games

    Where the numbers come from

    Stats come from Basketball-Reference through the Kaggle dataset “NBA Stats (1947-present)” by Sumitro Datta, covering every season from 1979-80 on (and every draft back to 1989). Headshots come from NBA.com and Basketball-Reference.

    Win Shares and Box Plus/Minus, the numbers behind several games, are Basketball-Reference's all-in-one estimates of how much a player helped his team win.

    How it's built

    The site is plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with no frameworks and no server. Python scripts (pandas) turn the raw CSVs into small JSON files the games load. Daily puzzles come from the date itself, so everyone gets the same puzzle without a server, and Hoop Connections checks every grid so each player fits exactly one group.

    It's hosted free on GitHub Pages, works offline once installed, and counts visits anonymously with GoatCounter (no cookies). The code is on GitHub.
